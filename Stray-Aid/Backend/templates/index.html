About StrayAid 🐾

StrayAid is an AI-powered animal rescue platform built to help volunteers, NGOs, and everyday citizens identify, report, and rescue stray animals across India — faster and smarter.

🤖 AI-Powered Detection 📍 Location-Aware 🤝 NGO Connected 📲 WhatsApp Reporting

Our Mission

1

Spot & Identify

Upload a photo of any stray animal — our AI model instantly identifies the species and screens for visible diseases or health conditions.

2

Cross-Verify with Gemini

Google Gemini Vision independently analyses the same image to cross-check our model's findings — giving you a second AI opinion for greater confidence.

3

Connect to Rescue

We locate nearby NGOs and animal welfare agencies on a live map, and generate a WhatsApp-ready report you can send in one tap.

4

Track & Follow Up

Every reported animal is logged to the dashboard so the community can track rescue status, treatment, and outcomes over time.

🧠

Dual AI System

Our custom-trained PyTorch model handles animal classification and disease detection. Google Gemini Vision acts as an independent cross-check, flagging any disagreement so you always get the most reliable result.

📍

Location Intelligence

Using your GPS or a manually entered address, we find the nearest animal NGOs and government vet departments on an interactive OpenStreetMap — with distance estimates and direct phone numbers.

📲

Instant WhatsApp Reports

Every analysis auto-generates a structured rescue report — animal type, condition, GPS coordinates, and Gemini's assessment — ready to send to any NGO or rescuer via WhatsApp in one click.

🏥

Disease Screening

Our disease models are trained separately for dogs, cats, and cows — detecting conditions like Ringworm, Demodicosis, Lumpy Skin Disease, Fungal Infections, and more from a simple photo.
